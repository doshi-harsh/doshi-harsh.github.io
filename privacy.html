Privacy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Who I am

This is the personal website of Harsh Doshi, Ottawa, Canada. You can reach me at me@iamharshdoshi.com.

What I collect

By default, nothing. The site has no accounts, forms or advertising. If you click Accept on the cookie notice, I load Google Analytics to see which pages are useful. It collects:

  • the pages you view and how long you stay;
  • your approximate location (country and city), device type, browser and the site you came from;
  • a random identifier stored in a cookie, so repeat visits can be counted.

I do not use Google Analytics for advertising. Ad personalisation and Google signals are switched off.
